A Story Called Malak — Birthday Website

To preview:
1. Extract this folder.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser (Chrome/Edge).
3. Keep the assets folder beside index.html.

To publish and share a link:
Upload the entire A_Story_Called_Malak folder to a static hosting service such as Netlify Drop.
Do not upload index.html alone; the photos, videos, and song are in assets/.

The music starts only after the visitor presses the play button, as browsers block autoplay.
